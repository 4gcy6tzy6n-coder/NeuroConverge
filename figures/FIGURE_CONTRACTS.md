## Shared rendering rules

- Keep biological literature, source-data reanalysis, model-derived weights, and synthetic tasks visually distinct.
- Name independent units on the panel; neurons nested in one specimen are not animals, and sessions are not animals.
- Show all decision-relevant comparators, including the result that weakens the focal claim.
- Show confidence/uncertainty only at the resampling unit used by the source study; do not invent cross-study intervals.
- Label post-result or exploratory analyses in the panel/legend when they change how the result should be read.
- Cite the source commit and paper-level data table in each legend after artifact and licensing audit.
