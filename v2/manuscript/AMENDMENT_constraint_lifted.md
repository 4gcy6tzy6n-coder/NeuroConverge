thirty-five rounds, and during them everything a manuscript needs except the prose was produced.**

**What changed is that the prose became the only remaining item this line could produce at all.** **The other
two items are external:**

| item | status |
| --- | --- |
| **an independent reviewer** | **still owed**, since round 10; `PREFLIGHT_REVIEW.md` records the review performed is this line's own |
| **the second-dataset generalisation test** | **closed in round 48** on infrastructure, with 1.866 GB spent and zero usable files |

**So the choice was between a manuscript drafted by this line and no manuscript.** **The owner chose the
former.**

## 4. The venue question, which the lift does not answer

**The lift authorises a manuscript; it does not decide where it is submitted.** **That decision changes the
claim wording, and this line cannot make it.** **The three live options and what each implies are recorded in
`../wp5_governance/SUBMISSION_READINESS_v2.md` section 4:** **NMI would require the machine-intelligence angle
to be foregrounded — derived datasets as inputs to learned models, and what their unstated unit, reliability
and specification do to the learned result; a methods venue would make the audit procedure the claim; and a
specialist neuroscience venue would make the atlas the claim.**

**The draft in `v2/manuscript/` is written to be re-aimable at any of the three, and does not commit to one.**
