# Claim ceiling

## Synthesis claim available for drafting

The two projects provide heterogeneous, bounded evidence that biological structure, biological computation, and artificial utility are distinct empirical questions. NeuroMotif's tested structure-to-dynamics results do not establish a general connectome-derived computational advantage. NeuroMech contains source-grounded biological findings and task-specific artificial studies, with local contrasts that coexist with stronger generic or task-aware controls, failed viability gates, or information/task correspondence limits. The portfolio motivates prospective tests of correspondence; it does not validate a general transfer law.

## Claims not currently licensed

- “Biological structure is insufficient” as a universal statement.
- “Biological mechanisms improve AI” as a general result.
- That the two projects identify one common algorithm, causal motif, or shared effect size.
- That a blocked public-data analysis is a negative biological result.
- That synthetic controller/learning outcomes validate the source biology.
- That any positive local ablation contrast establishes an overall or transferable advantage.
- That the proposed evidence hierarchy is a validated universal law or instrument.

Each Results claim must be bounded by its actual source, independent unit, frozen criterion, task, comparator, outcome history and reproduceability evidence. “Completed project” is not synonymous with a positive or fully reproducible result.
