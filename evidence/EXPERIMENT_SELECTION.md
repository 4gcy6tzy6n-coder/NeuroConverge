# Experiment selection for the main text (provisional)

The proposed four-result structure is an editorial scaffold, not an adjudicated final selection. Include a study only when its canonical contract, result, unit, and claim ceiling can be linked and reviewed.

1. **Structure and dynamics boundary:** NeuroMotif whole-connectome assay plus Fish1.5 E1 only with the one-specimen unit and invalid/indeterminate frozen null stated. Keep published worm evidence and blocked project reanalysis separate.
2. **Source-supported computation:** NeuroMech worm motor-state feedback and cerebellar interval-learning source analyses, carefully separating literature claims, project reanalysis, source-model simulations and synthetic implementation.
3. **Artificial utility:** candidate M2 and M5 results only as separate task-specific cases, including generic/replay/BPTT/task-aware comparators and any failed viability or information gates.
4. **Transfer framework:** synthesize only as a retrospective organizing proposal. Label each link and uncertainty; do not imply the sequence is a causal law or validated scale.

M11, Drosophila counterevidence, M12, M1/RR19, RR18, candidate screens, invalid runs and robustness results require explicit disposition in supplementary records. Do not fold them into a common effect or hide them solely because they complicate the narrative.
