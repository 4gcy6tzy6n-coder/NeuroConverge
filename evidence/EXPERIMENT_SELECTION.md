# Main-text evidence selection (internal manuscript draft)

The current four-Result structure is an internal evidence selection, not final publication adjudication. Each included claim is linked to its unit and claim ceiling; some historical source matches and owner-approved freeze decisions remain unresolved.

1. **Structure and dynamics boundary:** bounded whole-connectome assay plus single-specimen Fish1.5, with noise-floor crossover and invalid frozen topology null visible.
2. **Source-supported computation:** published worm motor-state feedback, schema-blocked project reanalysis, and cerebellar source-readout evidence. Separate measured activity, modeled weights and artificial rules.
3. **M2 V1:** synthetic post-actuator execution-sign access versus action-conditioned AcRKN, with information-recovery uncertainty, GRU and task-aware references.
4. **M5 V1:** aligned versus broken temporal correspondence, with generic ridge and replay; report the feature-matching/head-objective limitation in main text.

The transfer evidence map belongs in Discussion as a proposal. It is not a fifth empirical Result or a validated hierarchy. The selection and paragraph audit is in `../manuscript/RESULTS_RESTRUCTURE_AUDIT.md`. Historical placement, CF-LTD interval, XOR and generator studies are retained in Supplementary Results S2; their exact source crosswalk gaps remain explicit.

M11, Drosophila counterevidence, M12, M1/RR19, RR18, candidate screens, invalid runs and robustness results require explicit disposition in supplementary records. Do not fold them into a common effect or hide them solely because they complicate the narrative.


## Post-authorization V1 closure (1 October 2026)

The two explicitly authorized prospective synthetic tests are complete and independently checked. M2 did not show an AcRKN realized-state benefit over action-conditioned inference in its frozen task; M5 showed a fixed local rule used preserved temporal correspondence, while the generic same-feature ridge and exact replay effects were larger. These results are separate, synthetic-only extensions and are now documented in `new_experiments/`. They do not resolve the source-branch crosswalk or make the paper NMI-ready. The V1 experiment program is closed; variants require a separate scope decision.
