# NeuroConverge

**Integrating Connectome Structure, Neural Computation, and Artificial Intelligence**

Working paper title: **Evidence boundaries in transferring biological computation to artificial systems**

NeuroConverge is a publication-level synthesis of two source projects, extended on 9 October 2026 to include a third, separately governed engineering programme. NeuroMotif contributes bounded structure-to-dynamics evidence; NeuroMech contributes source-grounded mechanism and artificial-transfer studies. The purpose is to test what the combined record supports, while keeping distinct studies and their evidence classes separate. This repository does not merge source Git histories or replace either source of record.

For a concise map of the full project and its two workstreams, see [PROJECT_UNDERSTANDING.md](PROJECT_UNDERSTANDING.md).

## Central question

Which evidential transitions in neural-to-artificial transfer require separate empirical support?

The current evidence does not establish that connectome structure is universally insufficient, nor that biological computation generally improves AI. It supports a narrower retrospective synthesis: in the tested NeuroMotif assays, the proposed structural advantage was not established; in NeuroMech, biological source evidence and artificial results are separable, and transfer effects depend on the tested task, information available to the model, and comparator. This project-level framing remains a hypothesis-generating synthesis, not a validated universal law.

## Current status

**No new experiments are on the default path; technical interpretation, evidence traceability and submission readiness remain open.** On 9 October 2026 the owner authorized widening this manuscript to a **third workstream**, the IRCN budget-matched scheduling line, which had previously declared the other two programmes as read-only adjacent workspaces outside its inputs. The crossing is recorded as an owner-authorized scope amendment in the manuscript (Results 7, Methods), the supplementary (Methods S6, Table S11, Results S8), the claim matrix (C12–C15) and `evidence/IRCN_EVIDENCE_DISPOSITION.json`; neither declared boundary is rewritten. **The third line contributes no positive result.** Its case class is a comparison contract that could not be instantiated: the frozen eligibility rule required each scheduler control to come within one per cent of the treatment's own realized operation count on every pilot seed, so no primary baseline could be selected, the confirmatory seed count is zero and no confirmatory evaluation ran. M2, M5, DMP and Prospective V1 retain their frozen outputs. The manuscript now separates the V1 numeric interaction, reversed prose interpretation and failed conjunctive gate; it distinguishes the frozen prevalence-reference criterion from evidence of contextual learning; and it identifies DMP scaling as confounded with optimizer-update count. The recovered M0 files support only a Step-2/3/3A calibration/falsification sequence: the planned M0 pilot and T00–T11 main experiment are not documented as completed, and raw-to-result reconstruction is incomplete. The case-level contribution is articulated, but its sufficiency for *Nature Machine Intelligence* remains an open editorial judgment. Source freeze, crosswalk adjudication, rights/access, authorship declarations and final narrative review also remain open.
