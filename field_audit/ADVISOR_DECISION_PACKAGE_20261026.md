## Question 1 - Is the composition contrast described accurately?

**Measured** (`T2_CONVENTION_COMPOSITION_20261026.md`): the AI weaker-merge T2-E2 set and coder
H's T2-E2 set overlap on 1/16 primary (union 6), 2/42 validation (union 13); pooled 3 of 19
(15.8 percent). At T3 the two readings' E2 sets nest (H's is a strict subset of the AI's in both
corpora) - the operationalization sensitivity is T2-specific. Under the union of both
definitions, papers with ANY designed correspondence check are 6/16 and 13/42 (< 38 percent).

**Current manuscript wording (abstract + Results lead):** "The finding is a stable asymmetry:
controlled evidence concentrates where review already looks, utility comparisons (10/16 and
25/42), while the correspondence transition ... rests mostly on suggestive-only support (12/16
and 27/42)."

**Candidate wording (Results, replacing the asymmetry lead):**
> Controlled evidence concentrates at the utility transition under every reading (AI merge
> 10/16 and 25/42; independent human coder 4/16 and 19/42, a strict subset). At the
> correspondence transition the picture depends on what counts as a controlled test: a
> component-ablation definition (the AI rounds) and a biological-target-comparison definition
> (the human coder) each identify a minority (2/16 and 9/42 versus 5/16 and 6/42) and agree on
> only 3 of the 19 papers they jointly count. Under either definition, and under their union
> (6/16, 13/42), controlled correspondence evidence is the minority; on the validation corpus
> the utility-over-correspondence asymmetry holds under every reading, on the primary corpus it
> holds under the AI and the disagreement-handled readings and reverses under the human coder's
> raw reading. The sensitivity itself is a finding: the field has no shared operationalization
> of "this artifact implements that computation", which is the reporting gap the standard below
> closes.

**Abstract candidate (within the 150-word budget; replaces "The finding is a stable asymmetry..."
through "(12/16 and 27/42)."):**
> Controlled evidence concentrates at utility comparisons under every reading; at the
> correspondence transition, two defensible definitions of a controlled test each select a
> minority and agree on 3 of 19 papers, so the utility-correspondence asymmetry holds on the
> validation corpus under all readings and is convention-dependent on the primary corpus.

## Question 2 - Does the headline interval avoid over-generalization?

**Measured** (`O5_H3_RESULTS_20261025.md` section 7): headline E2+ by reading - primary 62.5 /
31.2 / 25.0 percent; validation 61.9 / 48.8 / 46.3 percent (AI merge / raw H / weaker(H, AI)).
Kill rule never triggered. Tail (E0/E1) ranges primary 37.5-75.0, validation 38.1-53.7 percent.

**Current wording:** "A preregistered kill rule (80 percent) was not triggered (62.5 and 61.9
percent)." - reports the AI-merge point only.

**Candidate wording (Results):**
> At the headline transition, the share of claims with controlled in-paper evidence is 62.5
> percent under the AI weaker merge (primary) and 61.9 percent (validation); the independent
> human coder's raw reading is lower (31.2 and 48.8 percent), and the preregistered
> disagreement-handled reading lower still (25.0 and 46.3 percent). The preregistered kill rule
> (80 percent) is not triggered under any reading; the unsupported tail is therefore between
> roughly two-fifths and three-quarters of claims depending on the reading, and never below
> 37.5 percent.

**Abstract candidate:** "A preregistered kill rule (80 percent) was not triggered under any
reading (controlled headline evidence 25-62.5 percent across coders and corpora)."

## Question 3 - Is the human coder's identity and participation adequately disclosed?

**Facts:** coder H = researcher Yang; human-primary; AI assisted material construction (text
extraction, formatting, mechanical checks, arithmetic summaries); final judgments by Yang; blind
to all AI grades and results (packet-only, no repository access); independence signature:
40 percent cell-level disagreement with the AI merge, matched-neither on 21/58 AI-disputed
cells, divergence against the project's own narrative. Scope: primary 16 x T1-T5 (80 cells),
validation 42 x T2/T3/T5 (126 cells); T1/T4 not coded on validation.

**Candidate Methods paragraph (new, "Independent human coding"):**
> After the two AI coding rounds, an independent human coder (Y.; author contribution stated
> below) graded the primary corpus in full (16 claims x 5 transitions) and the validation corpus
> on T2, T3 and T5 (42 x 3), from the pinned texts and the frozen instructions only, without
> access to any prior grade, merged sheet, adjudication or result. AI assistance in this pass was
> confined to material construction (text extraction, formatting, integrity checks, arithmetic
> summaries); every grade, headline choice and scope verdict is the coder's. The human coder's
> raw grades are reported as a reading in their own right (Results), beside the AI weaker merge
> and a preregistered disagreement-handled merge of the two; agreement with the AI merge was 61.2
> percent (primary) and 58.7 percent (validation) at the cell level, and on AI-disputed cells the
> human coder matched neither AI coder in 21 of 58.

**Disclosure placements:** Methods (above); Author contributions (Y.: independent human coding);
AI-assistance statement (existing, extended with the H-pass scope).
