# T2 convention composition, measured (2026-10-26): the paper's composition contrast, quantified

Authority: prepared for the advisor's science-expression decision 1 ("is the composition contrast
described accurately?"). The contrast has so far been asserted from spot-checks; this record
measures it from the committed sheets. No grade is changed; this is arithmetic on frozen data.

## The two operationalizations, as instantiated by the two readings

- **AI weaker-merge T2-E2 set** (component-ablation anchor): papers where the weaker merged grade
  at T2 is E2 - in practice, papers carrying a designed test of the mapped artificial component.
- **H T2-E2 set** (biological-target anchor): papers coder H graded E2 at T2 - in practice, papers
  comparing the artificial component's behavior against a biological target or surrogate
  (fixations vs subjects, output curve vs the cited Drosophila experiment, ANN tracking CANN,
  oracle tests, eye-tracking hit rates).

**T3 control (the same measurement on the utility transition):** the two readings' T3-E2 sets
overlap heavily (primary 10 vs 4 with the 4 a subset of the 10; validation 25 vs 19 with the 19
a subset of the 25). H's T3 set is strictly contained in the AI set in both corpora. The
disagreement is thus T2-specific: the correspondence construct is where operationalization
matters; the utility construct is robust to coder identity. This is exactly the shape the

## The per-claim union view (the constructive deliverable)

Union T2-E2 across both readings: 6 of 16 primary (37.5 percent), 13 of 42 validation (31.0
percent). Under either single convention the share is 12.5-31.2 percent; under the union it
rises. A paper with ANY designed correspondence check - component ablation or biological-target
comparison - remains the minority, and the reporting standard's T2 item is what would make the
two types distinguishable at publication time. This gives the manuscript a precise, convention-
robust sentence: "under either definition, controlled correspondence evidence is the minority
(the two definitions agree on only 3 of 19 papers they count); under their union, still under
38 percent in both corpora."
