# Novelty scan protocol (frozen 2026-10-25, before searches run)

Purpose: the NMI Article framing claims the audit instrument is a novel research contribution.
This scan freezes, BEFORE any search output exists, what would count as a competitor, so the
novelty claim is tested rather than asserted. Feeds the manuscript's related-work positioning;
the scan's failure class is finding a class-A instrument, which would force repositioning.

## Comparability classes

- **A (direct competitor).** A published instrument that grades the evidence behind
  bio-to-AI / neuroscience-inspired AI transfer claims at the level of an inference CHAIN
  (transition-level, not paper-level), with or without a reporting layer. Finding one changes
  the manuscript's contribution claim from "instrument" to "replication/extension".
- **B (adjacent, AI meta-science).** Paper-level instruments in ML/AI meta-science:
  reproducibility checklists (NeurIPS/RLReplicates), claims-vs-evidence audits, leakage
  taxonomies (Kapoor & Narayanan 2023, NMI), statistical-precipice reporting, dataset audits
  (Longpre et al. 2024, NMI). Expected to exist; the manuscript must position against them.
  None decomposes a claim into biology-to-utility transitions; that difference is checkable.
- **C (plausibility critiques).** Arguments that "biological plausibility" is under-defined
  (Guest & Martin 2023-style) or analyses of brain-likeness of representations. Critiques
  without a measurement instrument are not competitors; they are motivation.
- **D (evidence grading outside AI).** GRADE (clinical medicine), certainty-of-evidence
  frameworks, systematic-review standards. The CONCEPT of evidence grading is old and must be
  credited; the claim is the transition-level decomposition + evidence/reporting separation +
  executable scorer for THIS literature.
- **E (surveys of neuro-AI).** Hassabis et al. 2017-style inspiration mappings and recent
  brain-as-blueprint surveys (two are inside our own corpus). Surveys organize; they do not
  grade evidence per transition.

## Search plan (executed 2026-10-25, queries recorded verbatim in the scan record)

Web searches across: transition/chain evidence grading + brain-inspired; evidence-quality
frameworks for AI claims; ML reproducibility/checklist instruments; plausibility-definition
critiques; GRADE-family applied to ML. Snowball one level from any class-A or strong class-B
hit. Verdicts per class with links; absence of hits is recorded as absence on these queries at
this date, not proof of nonexistence.

## Output

`NOVELTY_SCAN_20261025.md`: per-class verdicts, sources, and the honest novelty statement the
manuscript may carry. If class A is found: stop and reposition before any novelty claim enters
the manuscript.
